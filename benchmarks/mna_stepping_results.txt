# MNA stepping: before and after issue #21

Measured on one machine, one after the other with nothing else running (`--steps 50 --rounds 5`).
Timings depend on the machine and its load: they are recorded here, never asserted in a test.

| case | before (ms/step) | after (ms/step) | speed-up |
|---|---|---|---|
| RCModel | 6.70 | 2.17 | 3.1x |
| HeatingRectifier | 55.87 | 23.34 | 2.4x |
| case118 (one solve) | 51.79 | 26.80 | 1.9x |

What changed: linear layers take the full Newton step at once (RCModel: 2 -> 1 Jacobian per
step); the Jacobian is coloured in reverse mode (PyTorch's forward-mode rules for elementwise
ops run through torch._refs, about 80x an op's own cost); per-layer tolerances let the
HeatingRectifier's thermal layer stop at its own round-off floor (14.2 -> 10.6 Jacobians per
step).

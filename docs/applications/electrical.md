# Electrical circuits

`noodl.apps.electrical` models electrical circuits: node voltages (V) and branch currents (A)
solved by Modified Nodal Analysis in a `PotentialFlowLayer`, in DC, transient and phasor (AC)
modes. Its device laws (resistance, capacitance, inductance, voltage and current sources with
their waveforms, the junction diode, the switch, the ideal transformer, coupled inductors and
the four controlled sources) follow the Modelica Standard Library; every name is listed in the
[catalogue](../catalogue/electrical.md).
